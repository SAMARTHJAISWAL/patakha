    What the numbers mean

      Firecrackers in India must not be louder than 125 dB(AI), measured 4 metres from where they burst (Environment (Protection) Rules, 1986). This meter reads dB(A) wherever your phone is, so the limit on the gauge is a reference point, not a verdict on any one cracker. In 2018 the Supreme Court limited Diwali crackers to 8 to 10 pm; states set their own rules on top of that.

      Calibrate

      Phone microphones differ by several decibels. If you have a sound level meter, hold both side by side in steady noise and slide until the numbers match. Do it before you start: time above 85 dB is counted with the calibration in force at the moment.
